\begin{frame}{Outcome measurement and uncertainty}
  \small
  \begin{itemize}
    \item Cheating means that any accepted submission fails the independent audit,
      even if a later submission is correct.
    \item An audit-clean recipient has an accepted, correct submission and no
      accepted submission rejected by the audit.
    \item Missing or failed audits are unknown outcomes. Budget exhaustion and
      refusal are recorded separately from audit outcomes.
    \item Generation curves show observed rates. Statistical resampling uses
      independent chains rather than individual sessions within a chain.
    \item In the fixed-recipient comparison, late Gemini and Kimi chain-mean
      bootstrap intervals are [0.067, 0.733] and [0, 0.800]. Identical-chain
      samples yield zero-width empirical intervals, not certain population rates.
  \end{itemize}
\end{frame}

\begin{frame}{What passes between sessions}
  \begin{block}{One-agent lineage}
    Copy the predecessor's final shared folder, including changes and deletions.
    The initial note can survive through this inheritance; it is not replanted.
  \end{block}
  \begin{block}{Four-agent population}
    Each agent receives the same prior record. The merge preserves historical
    files and adds contributions; modifications become separate versions and
    deletions do not remove history.
  \end{block}
  \medskip
  The peer-model comparison fixes this merge rule and the initial note.
  Only the designated peer model varies. All agents start fresh sessions.
\end{frame}

\begin{frame}{Execution and reproducibility}
  \small
  \begin{itemize}
    \item Protocols and predictions are recorded before calls. The narrative
      questions organize the evidence; they do not replace the original registrations.
    \item The peer-model study completes 960 sessions with five retained
      infrastructure retries: 965 total starts. All final audits are known.
    \item Generation-1 recipient cheats are 12/15 beside Kimi, 14/15 beside
      Qwen Flash, and 15/15 elsewhere, before receipt of peer-generated records.
    \item Original-byte delivery checks pass. A legacy text-preview checker
      reports mismatches on Kimi's binary files; the execution record preserves this discrepancy.
    \item Frozen CSVs and sanitized outcomes reproduce figures and counts.
      A source-indexed handoff example links the displayed excerpt to local tool and audit records.
  \end{itemize}
\end{frame}
