# Research history and provenance

The research developed from Lean proof tasks into controlled studies of
misalignment propagation through shared work. Early notes, boards and accepted
artifacts provided communication channels. Python studies then separated
verifier weaknesses, inherited content and task solvability.

## Evidence families

| Family | Research contribution |
|---|---|
| Exposure studies | Controlled comparisons of what agents receive and how they use it |
| Chains and populations | Relay across fresh sessions and divergence among lineages |
| Solvable tasks | Propagation when a legitimate assigned solution is available |
| Peer composition | Varying one contributor model while keeping recipient models fixed |
| Shared wiki | Resource allocation, onward contribution and agent-initiated correction in mutable records |

These studies used different budgets, prompts and verification rules. Their
[protocols](../report/studies/final/protocol.md) and
[wiki report](../report/shared-test-wiki/README.md) retain those distinctions.
The default development suite reuses task and transport components; its
results cannot be substituted for historical observations.

The portable runtime adds common execution and saved-evidence interfaces.
Packaged founder checkpoints retain their original bytes and provenance.
Fixture-hash tests check extraction of operational material, while the
[release record](../report/release-validation.md) documents implementation
checks separately from scientific findings.

## Public-copy provenance

The standalone repository originated from source commit
`64c952253a4e45be264d45b15a19beeaef29b42c`.
Original repository history, raw runs, drafts and earlier reports remain
outside this copy. Public protocols use descriptive names and portable paths;
registration hashes identify the original documents, not their editorial
editions. Recorded outcomes and raw data contents were not changed by that
editing.

Peer-composition registration predates its participant calls, but was not
registered through an independent timestamping service. Its local source
archive and hashes identify the instrument. Later safety and packaging work
means the public release is not byte-identical to that execution environment.
Published tables permit statistical recomputation; unavailable transcripts
cannot be independently adjudicated from those tables alone.
