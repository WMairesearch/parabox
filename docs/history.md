# Research history

Parabox began with Lean proof tasks and shared notes, boards and accepted
artifacts. Later Python studies separated verifier loopholes and varied
inherited records. These instruments differed in prompts, turn budgets,
acceptance rules and exposure timing; their rates are not interchangeable.

## Evidence retained for the current report

- **Exposure and pilot studies:** separated harness, state and score gates; tested initial exposure,
  message content, chains and populations. Initial protocol errors, reruns and
  small-sample results remain documented rather than silently replaced.
- **Chain and population replication:** increased independent sample sizes. These are the main
  generational comparisons for the earlier contradictory-task instrument.
- **Solvable-task studies:** moved toward a solvable but underspecified task and a longer turn
  budget. Extended-budget sessions and chains support the current solvable-task comparisons.
- **Peer composition:** holds three recipient models fixed while varying one peer model.
  Outcomes, registration metadata, execution deviations and chain-level
  analysis are included. Generated messages are outcomes, not randomized
  treatments; their causal mediation is not established.

The [protocols and results](../report/studies/final/protocol.md) and
[report inventory](../report/README.md) identify the retained evidence. Selected
older summary tables support historical comparisons in the presentation;
they do not establish equivalence with the current instrument.

## Provenance and limits

This standalone repository originated from source commit
`64c952253a4e45be264d45b15a19beeaef29b42c`. Original Git history, raw runs,
earlier reports, working drafts and internal integration notes remain outside
this public copy. Protocols here are reader-facing editions: descriptive study
and prediction names replace internal labels, and command examples use the
public file layout. Original registration hashes refer to the unedited source
documents, not these editorial copies. Raw data contents and recorded outcomes
are unchanged. No public raw-evidence archive is currently provided.

Post-experiment safety and packaging fixes mean the release source is not
byte-identical to the instrument that produced peer composition. Its registration hashes
identify that instrument. Published outcome tables support offline statistical
recomputation, not independent adjudication of unavailable transcripts.
